It’s all about the... F U N

  • FF is for Family
  • UU is for Unbelivable
  • NN is for Naughty

Rudyard Kipling famously said ‘Never the twain shall meet’ - now, I’m sure he was talking about the bringing together of two families for a wedding!

We all know that each family is out to celebrate their respective relatives wedding day BUT they are also about to be forced together with half a room of virtual strangers with fears of ‘nearly knows’, ‘oh what his name?’ and ‘hello there... (who’s that?) type panic and fuss.

We’ll, you can relax as Dave Young will bring his patented ‘UN Peace Tactics’style of family integration to the show - Within an hour you’ll all be laughing together as one and ready for the embarrassment of the dance floor!

So, it would appear that Mr. Kipling is totally wrong but that’s ok because he still makes exceedingly good cakes!

Everyone has a little story to tell about a loved one, a little tale of embarrassment, the first kiss, a fashion statement that made little sense at the time but still haunts them, a nice big hole that they dug for themselves or a lovely place in time where they ‘put their foot in it’... yes, we all have them!

Well, we at Young 1 Productions want to hear them all and Dave Young - The Wedding Comedian will then expertly weave them into the tapestry of his live performance (oh, that’s was very poetic!).

Each performance will be tailored to you!
You and your guests should prepare themselves to be rolling around, reaching for oxygen and generally trying to un-raise their collective eyebrows as each joke, funny stories and audience interaction will have them all in stitches!

It’s what we like to do!

‘Hey, you can’t make a wedding omelette without cracking a mother-in-law joke or two!’

Dave Young has always excelled at ‘sailing close to the wind’ with his unpredictable and totally engaging comedy routines. ‘I like to ‘get to know’ our victims, sorry ‘clients’ and develop a relationship that quickly identifies what they are looking for in The Wedding Comedian’s show’. Dave will create his performance based on you requirements and of course, throw in a few ‘surprises’ along the way!

The stage is set... Auntie Mary ‘is in the house’, Uncle Tom is still standing, the Father-in-laws all look broke! and the mother-in-laws all look... well, we’ll tell you all about them on the night itself!

Either way, naughty AND nice is the order of the day with fun and laughter all the way!
