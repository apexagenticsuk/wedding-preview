How to edit your website

Sign in

Open this page, enter your passcode and tap Sign in. Demo mode lets you try everything without changing the real site.

Change words

Pick a page at the top. Things you can change get a yellow outline when you point at them. Click a heading, paragraph or button and just type. Click somewhere else when you're done.

Change a button

Click any button or link. A panel opens on the right where you can change its words, where it goes (one of your pages, a web address, a phone number or an email) and its colour.

Change a date, review, video or answer

Click a review, video, FAQ, blog post or the contact box to open Shared content: each one is written once and shows everywhere it is used. Type a blog post date any normal way, for example Fri 4th Dec, 4 Dec 2026 or 04/12/2026: the weekday is ignored, and a message tells you if a date can't be read. Rows have ↑ ↓ to reorder, Delete, and + Add is under the list.

Swap the poster or a photo

Click any picture and choose Replace picture. Photos from your phone are shrunk automatically.

Move or hide a section

Drag: point at a section and grab the blue ⠿ Drag tab (top right), or a block’s yellow ⠿, and drop it where the yellow line shows; the page scrolls when you get near the edge. Pictures, cards, testimonials and other repeated items have a small ⠿ too (on a phone, tap the section first). Drag menu links sideways, or the ⠿ in Pages. In Add a block you can drag a tile straight onto the page (on a phone: hold it, then drag). Every drag can be undone with ↶.

Or click inside a section: the bar under the toolbar shows what is selected, with Move up, Move down, Reorder… (drag in a list), Hide, Copy and Delete. Hidden sections stay faded in the editor so you can bring them back.

Check, then publish

📱 Phone shows how it looks on a mobile. Preview opens the site with your changes. When you're happy, tap the big red Publish. The real website updates in about a minute.

Add something new

Click any heading or paragraph and tap the yellow + under it (or + Add below) to add a heading, text, picture, button, video, gallery, quote, cards and more. Use the blue + Add section lines between sections for ready-made layouts.

Add or change pages

Tap Pages at the top. + New page makes a new page from a ready-made layout and adds it to the menu. From there you can also rename, copy, delete, hide from the menu, change the menu order and the Google title and description.

Made a mistake?

Use ↶ Undo. Unpublished changes are kept even if you close the page. After you publish, the version before it is kept. To undo a publish, open Help and press Go back one version at the top.
